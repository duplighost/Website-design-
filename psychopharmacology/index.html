// the method

Convergence, not authority

mechanism → trials → experts → prescribers → patients

Mechanism explains what should happen. Trials show what can happen under controlled conditions. Experts recognize patterns. Prescribers see what survives real life. Patients reveal whether the result is actually livable. No single one of them gets the last word.

The truth is the fit.
